# 3D Assets & Procedural Models Documentation

## Overview
All 3D assets in **SQIFTREE RUN ("The House")** are lightweight procedural geometries, CC0-inspired low-poly architectural designs, and original toon-shaded models rendered using Three.js and `@react-three/fiber`.

Total 3D asset footprint: **< 1 MB** (zero heavy external binary downloads, fully optimized for immediate loading on integrated GPUs and mobile devices).

---

## 1. Character "sqiftree"
- **Design:** Original concept — young explorer in long dark frontier-modern trench coat, rolled sleeves, cargo pants, trail boots, and gold accent trim.
- **Copyright & IP Compliance:** 100% original design. No copyrighted likenesses, logos, or characters from anime/commercial media.
- **Rendering:** Three.js procedural humanoid compound hierarchy with toon-shading materials and smooth animation interpolation.

---

## 2. Stand "KING KRULE"
- **Design:** Original translucent golden silhouette companion (+0.9, +0.4, -0.8 offset), elongated arms resembling guitar-neck barrels for Reverb Shot, bowed posture, twilight glow aura (`#C9A84C` / `#E8D49A`).
- **Copyright & IP Compliance:** Homage name only. Contains no copyrighted music samples, album artwork, or trademarks.
- **Abilities:**
  - *Reverb Shot:* Expanding ring waves fired toward interactive targets (0.25s cooldown).
  - *Echo Pulse:* 360° golden pulse revealing hidden hotspots through walls for 3s (8s cooldown).
  - *Dusk Dash:* Golden dust sprint trails with stamina meter.

---

## 3. Architecture & Furniture ("The House")
- **Layout:** 16m × 10m × 3m four-room compound:
  - **Room A (Workshop):** Telemetry dual-screen GCS workstation, hanging RC pusher-propeller plane, engineering tool bench.
  - **Room B (Web Studio):** Cashier POS terminal (Bingilo), village service map billboard (Desa Jetis), mobile test cradle (HaloMBG), UX sticky-card board (NEO).
  - **Room C (Profile & Contact):** Stand capability radar board, race log corkboard with ticket tags, brass mailbox.
  - **Room D (Lobby & Entrance):** Wooden credenza with vintage turntable record player, front door entrance.
- **Source & References:**
  - Inspired by open-source modular asset principles from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) (CC0 1.0 Universal) and [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com) (CC0 1.0 Universal).
  - Architectural styling: warm cream plaster (`#F5F2EE`), weathered wood trim, golden hour directional lighting (`#FFB070`), and charcoal accents (`#0D0D0D`).
